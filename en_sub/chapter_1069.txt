Although she knew Li Mu very well, she was also moved by Li Mu's speech to the point that her eyes turned red. She felt that her feelings today were very different from the others present. First of all, she was Li Mu's personal assistant. It could be said that she was the closest to Li Mu in terms of working level. Second, she was also a Hua Xia person. Seeing Li Mu create such glory at Harvard, her excitement was not something that any American college student present could feel.

Not only Li Ziwei, but every Chinese and Chinese student in the audience today had similar feelings as Li Ziwei. They were different from the students of other countries. Not only were they shocked, but they also felt extremely proud. This kind of pride and pride was far beyond that of the Olympic champions.

Zhen Xiaoxiao, who was squeezed in the middle of the stage with a few Chinese classmates, had already cried several times today. She had originally admired Li Mu, but because she had been studying abroad for the past few years, the Li Mu that she knew was no different from the Americans who knew Li Mu. It was nothing more than labels such as: young, rich, internet genius, YY founder, etc. However, today, she truly understood what kind of person Li Mu was. Especially when Li Mu mentioned 3321, she was extremely shocked and touched. She felt that Li Mu had overturned her worldview many times today.

Before today, if someone had asked her this question, she would have said, "How can young people nowadays have any sense of social responsibility?"

However, today, Li Mu had truly shown her what a true sense of social responsibility was. Not to mention young people, even those who had dominated the rich list for many years might not be able to match up to Li Mu in this aspect!

Secondly, she used to think that the rich people of the Western world were too great, donating hundreds of millions of US dollars at every turn. There were even some who made naked donations. However, it was only today that she realized that all of this was just a pretense. The charity funds of billionaires were just a tool to avoid the high inheritance tax. The inheritance tax was as high as 55%, and after "donating" to the charity fund, all the money would not be taxed, and the real money used for charity might not even be 5%!

This kind of sentiment was many times more noble than those hypocritical rich people.

Furthermore, from Li Mu's speech, she realized that Li Mu's real goal was not to make money, nor was it to make his business bigger and stronger. What he really wanted to do was extremely ambitious, because he wanted to change the world!

Countless young people had fantasized about changing the world, but it seemed that only Li Mu had done it, and he was far from being satisfied. This made Li Mu's charm in her eyes explode. From now on, in her eyes, there were only two ways to categorize the opposite sex. One was Li Mu, and the other was people other than Li Mu.

At this time, they hoped that Li Mu could come out and lift their hearts for a while longer.
