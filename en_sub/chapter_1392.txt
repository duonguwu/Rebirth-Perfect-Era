Chapter 1392

Two days later, Wei Lan, with a simple draft, took Wan Ying, Muye Yingxiang, and the relevant person in charge of Xiangnan Satellite TV to Miyun and had a closed-door meeting with the entire Miyun County Party Committee for several hours.

Before that, the Miyun County Party Committee also couldn't figure out why a general manager of a real estate company would bring people from Makino Technology's subsidiary companies and well-known staff of Xiangnan Satellite TV to discuss cooperation with them. For the current Miyun, the attention of the outside world was too weak. Not to mention large companies with a market value of hundreds of millions of yuan, even if a small entrepreneur worth millions of yuan wanted to invest in Miyun, they would also give a high degree of attention and attention.

And when the long meeting was over, all the local leaders present were immersed in a state of doubt about life and could not extricate themselves. They even wondered if it was a dream, because everything they heard at the meeting seemed so unreal.

Makino Image is going to open a variety show with Xiangnan Satellite TV, and is interested in selecting a location to shoot in Miyun?!

Wanying Real Estate is interested in launching a large-scale cultural tourism project with a scale of 4-5 billion yuan in Miyun?!

About the tourism resources of Miyun, not to mention Xiangnan Satellite TV, the top domestic satellite TV, even Yanjing Satellite TV is too lazy to come to report. However, Xiangnan Satellite TV is going to cooperate with Li Mu's company to do a long-term variety show in Miyun, and it is based on the natural scenery of Miyun. 12 + 1, this is 13 episodes!

Each episode is at least an hour, and all 13 episodes of the show revolve around Miyun. Even if it is only one episode, it will be equal to the media exposure of Miyun in the past ten or twenty years, right?

Moreover, a single investment of 5 billion yuan in a cultural tourism project has never been heard of in the whole country. It is still understandable in first-tier cities, but I dare not dream that Miyun can have such a large-scale tourism investment!

However, all the seemingly impossible things have magically happened in one day. This is the biggest breakthrough in Miyun's publicity, investment attraction, and tourism development in many years, which made all the parties involved extremely excited.

Subsequently, the County Party Committee urgently demonstrated the feasibility of the project.

Although everyone came to this demonstration with excitement, there were still many doubts or unconfident voices at the demonstration meeting.

For example, can such a large project really land in Miyun?

For example, did Wan Ying really have the ability to manage such a big project?

Another example, would such a project become an unfinished project halfway through, and then become famous throughout the country due to the huge scale of investment, becoming the largest unfinished cultural tourism industry in the country?

For example, even if it really landed, would it be able to make money?

What if the promotion was not good, or it was not attractive to tourists, and it became a "ghost town" in the tourism industry?

However, when faced with similar voices, the resolute replied with a single sentence, dispelling all the doubts in the hearts of the doubters.

What was there to be afraid of?

Li Mu is also one of the bosses of Wan Ying Group. At the end of the day, this is also a project that Li Mu has invested in. Moreover, Li Mu is the chief designer of the entire project. Are you still worried that Li Mu will make a lousy project and embarrass himself?

Are you worried that with Li Mu's influence, he won't be able to build a man-made ancient city?

If we don't even trust Li Mu, how many entrepreneurs can we trust?

If we carry this kind of mentality to attract investors, then the final result will be that we will not be able to attract business, nor will we be able to attract investors!

After figuring this out, everyone almost immediately stopped questioning. Although no one could guarantee that such a huge project could be successfully completed as planned, and no one could guarantee that such a project could still make money after it was completed, because of Li Mu's golden brand, there was really nothing to worry about. Li Mu was managing a project worth five billion yuan. Would anyone be afraid that he would make an unfinished project?

No way.

Therefore, in less than half an hour, the entire county committee passed the demonstration and came to a highly unanimous conclusion: Yes!

They spared no effort in supporting the project.

First, there was the problem of land.

Wei Lan wants the entire valley under the Simatai Great Wall. This piece of land is not arable land, nor is it a residential area, and it is not a factory that has changed owners. The County Party Committee has the right to decide on this piece of land, so this piece of land is not a problem. But as a county-level government, they must inform the Municipal Party Committee before they start this project. However, the possibility of the Municipal Party Committee stopping this project is zero.

In recent years, the country had been encouraging sustainable development and the tourism and cultural industries. If the government took out the land for industrial use, the operation would be more or less cumbersome. However, if the land was used for tourism, there would basically be no problem. This was because not only would it be beneficial to the development of the tourism industry, but it would also be of great help to the development of the local reputation and tourism potential. Every place dreamed of getting such a cultural tourism investment. In the eyes of the local government, this was much better than industrial investment!

Secondly, there was the problem of money. The land was used to attract investors and promote the development of the tourism industry. Therefore, the county committee could use the land as a preferential policy to attract investors. This way, Wanying Real Estate would not need to pay the land use fee.

Then, there was the issue of whether the county committee would invest. They had to invest, otherwise, the county committee would just be a bystander in this project. The county committee was willing to accept Wanying's proposal and invest in the Gubei Water Town project at a premium. They would set up a new company with Wanying to specialize in Wanying Gubei Water Town.

The only problem left was the loan. The county government promised Wan Ying that they would get a long-term, low-interest loan for the project through communication with the bank. If they couldn't get the loan, the county government would allocate a portion of their own funds to subsidize the project's interest rate. All in all, the actual interest paid by the project would be much lower than the interest paid by ordinary real estate developers.

According to Li Mu, Wanying Real Estate must hold on to 60% of the shares. The remaining 40% would be reserved for the state-owned tourism groups and the local government. Therefore, the specific proportion of the county committee's investment quota would depend on the next step of the negotiation between Wanying Real Estate and the state-owned tourism groups.

After communicating with the county government on all the issues that needed to be confirmed, Wei Lan happily prepared to negotiate with the state-owned tourism groups. Unexpectedly, the situation changed as soon as she returned to the city.

This change came from the municipal party committee. Because a project had a budget of 5 billion yuan, the scale was huge, so when the county party committee did the routine report, it received great attention from the municipal party committee. After the municipal party committee found Wan Ying Real Estate and Mu Ye Image to understand and confirm the authenticity of the project, they immediately became interested and attached great importance to it.

In the eyes of Li Mu and Wei Lan, this was a cultural tourism real estate project worth five billion yuan.

In the eyes of the Miyun County committee, this was a five billion yuan investment opportunity for the overall development of the tourism industry.

However, in the eyes of the municipal committee, it was not only a shocking cultural tourism project, but also an extremely bold and innovative attempt, which was of great significance.

So far, almost all the tourism projects in the country were developed based on the location. In other words, except for historical tourism projects, almost all natural tourism projects were 70% on the scenic spots and 30% on the development.

Why did they develop Mount Tai? It was because Mount Tai stood there and had good enough basic conditions.

Why did they develop Jiuzhai? It was because the scenery of Jiuzhai had been formed over thousands of years, and it was already impeccable.

The reason why Shennongjia, Mount Huang, and Guilin were developed was because they themselves had excellent foundations. With a little investment, they could become popular tourist attractions if they were transformed into tourist attractions.

To put it bluntly, the existing tourism industry relied on the weather to make a living. Only when God gave this land suitable resources for tourism development could it develop in the tourism industry. Otherwise, it would be a fool's dream to think about tourism.

This kind of tourism industry structure was actually the biggest headache for the government. Speaking of the vast Hua Xia, with 9.6 million square kilometers of land, there were many beautiful mountains, rivers, and scenery, but if you really looked at it as a whole, you would find that the ratio of tourism resources in the country was seriously unbalanced. Most cities and regions did not have natural tourism resources, and they could only watch the tourism industry in other places do well. How many cities in the country's annual tourism income could not even compare to the annual ticket price of a 4A scenic spot, let alone a 5A scenic spot?

If the industry model did not change and was not upgraded, then these cities and regions that did not have tourism resources or were slightly lacking in tourism resources would never be able to develop the tourism industry.

Now, the famous Li Mu actually wanted to get involved in the tourism industry, and what he wanted to do was to rely on 30% of the weather to make a living, and 70% of the matter depended on human effort. In the small ravine below Simatai in Miyun, honestly speaking, other than the Great Wall of Simatai which was slightly famous, there was no real tourism attraction at all. If Simatai was not included, there were at least hundreds of such valleys in the Yan Mountain Range in Yan Jing. In the eyes of traditional tourism developers, these hundreds of valleys had no room for development. The most important thing was that the basic conditions were not good enough.

However, Wanying Gubeishui Town was not like this. It was no longer a traditional tourism development. It needed to use the developer's mind, the developer's planning, and the developer's capital to turn a place with weak basic conditions into a huge cluster tourist attraction. In the domestic tourism industry, this was the first time. If such a tourism project could be successful, it would be a major breakthrough and innovation in the domestic tourism development industry, and it would bring new inspiration to those areas that did not have a good tourism foundation.

Therefore, the municipal party committee, which attached great importance to the Wanying Gubeishui Town project, immediately appointed the department leader in charge of tourism development to take the lead and form a project team with the Miyun County Party Committee to be responsible for the follow-up of this project.
