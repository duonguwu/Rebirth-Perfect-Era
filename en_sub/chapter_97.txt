Chapter 97

On the morning of August 17th, when the hit-and-run incident became public knowledge in Haizhou, a piece of shocking news was released on the Internet.

According to the news, the hit-and-run driver was Wu Dong, the boss of the Haizhou Coal Mine. On the night of the accident, Wu Dong had invited Guo Lin, the mine manager of the Xiling Coal Mine, for a drink. The reason was simple. Guo Lin was his god of wealth and the greatest guarantee of his wealth. After drinking, Wu Dong drove Guo Lin home and killed the victim couple on the way. The car involved in the accident had been shipped out of Haizhou overnight by Wu Dong, and he was going to change the car and destroy all the evidence.

Many inside stories were also exposed, such as the relationship between Guo Lin and Wu Dong. The reason Wu Dong was able to make a fortune was because he colluded with Guo Lin and gave Guo Lin a lot of benefits to monopolize the production of the Xiling Coal Mine. If it were not for Guo Lin, Wu Dong would not have been able to drive a BMW. If it were not for Guo Lin, the tragic accident would not have happened.

Zhao Kang thought that Li Mu was giving out fake news to take revenge on Guo Lin. However, he did not ask much. He just followed Li Mu's instructions and let his core Internet Water Army add fuel to the fire with the proxy IP in Haizhou.

When the news first came out, the public was skeptical. Although the news made sense and explained the external and internal causes, no one dared to fully believe it without any concrete evidence.

However, some enthusiastic netizens soon began to collect all kinds of evidence to prove the accuracy of the news.

First, someone who claimed to be Wu Dong's neighbor revealed that the imported BMW that Wu Dong had bought at the beginning of the year had disappeared. Unlike before, this car could be seen driving in and out of the house every day.

Then, many netizens said that Wu Dong was quite arrogant when he drove the BMW. Whether it was day or night, in the city, the suburbs, or on the national highway, people saw Wu Dong driving the BMW at high speed. His license plate number 58888 was also very eye-catching. Almost everyone in Haizhou knew about it. It was indeed suspicious that such a high-profile car had suddenly disappeared.

Then, someone said that Wu Dong was no longer driving a BMW when he went out for the past two days. Instead, his chauffeur drove an Audi to pick him up, which was out of the ordinary.

The most important piece of news was that someone met Wu Dong and Li Jiawei, the Deputy Director of the Municipal Public Security Bureau, in a luxurious fish restaurant in the suburbs on the second night of the accident. It was strange that they met the Deputy Director of the Public Security Bureau right after the accident.

A netizen who claimed to be a waiter at the fish manor said that the two of them had indeed eaten together that day.

With so many different pieces of evidence, the public's attitude changed drastically. They were no longer skeptical, but they were certain that Wu Dong was the culprit behind the accident!

After the public identified Wu Dong as the murderer, they immediately began to criticize him. Guo Lin, who was also said to have been in the car that night, was not spared.

Guo Lin was the mine manager of the Xiling Coal Mine, a corrupt official in the state-owned enterprise. He was the one who had sent more than 80% of the coal from the Xiling Coal Mine to Wu Dong. While helping Wu Dong earn a lot of money, he had also accepted a lot of bribes from Wu Dong. Not only did he abuse his power to take bribes and play with young women, but he also became an accomplice in the hit-and-run case. This was the key to the netizens' renewed anger toward him.

There was even a saying on the Internet that if Guo Lin had not been colluding with Wu Dong's officials and businessmen, Wu Dong would not have specially invited him to a banquet, let alone drunk driving after the banquet. Therefore, Guo Lin had to bear at least half of the responsibility for this tragedy.

With the help of the Internet Water Army, Guo Lin was once again pushed to the forefront by the netizens.

In Li Mu's eyes, Wu Dong's incident still needed the Internet Water Army to continue to heat up the netizens, and even spread the influence of this incident to Jinling. Once the higher-ups in the public security department paid attention to it, Wu Dong would be doomed. Although the rumor that Guo Lin was in the car at the time of the accident was slandered by Li Mu, Guo Lin could not defend himself. Everyone was not concerned about whether he was in the car or not, but about his relationship with Wu Dong.

At the very least, Li Mu wanted Guo Lin to be removed from his position as the mine manager of the Xiling Coal Mine. If he could get Guo Lin in, that would be even better.

Li Mu left the rest of the matter to Zhao Kang. He wanted Zhao Kang to continue to spread the news on the Pengcheng and Jinling forums, in case the local officials from Haizhou got involved. It would be even more interesting if he could alert the people from the province.

As for himself, he did not have the time to keep an eye on the situation, because there was a family dinner waiting for him at night.

His parents had booked a private room at Haizhou Restaurant. It was the same grandiose room 888 from last time, and the guests were relatives of Li Ma's family.

Papa Lee also wanted to treat the Li family to a meal at Haizhou Restaurant, but the Li family lived far away from the National People's Congress, so it would be too much for them to come to the city for a meal.

Only Li Mu's uncle and his family knew that Papa Lee had opened a store for Li Ma. When Papa Lee's uncle heard that his second sister and brother-in-law had offered his wife a salary of two thousand yuan, he was so surprised that he could not speak. After confirming it several times, he finally understood that although his second sister and brother-in-law were honest, his nephew was really extraordinary. He usually did not show himself, but he had actually earned a huge amount of wealth in a short period of time. He had even opened such a large-scale exclusive store for his parents.

However, Papa Lee's uncle knew that he could not tell others about this. As a result, Li Mu's grandparents, who lived with him, knew nothing about this matter. They only heard that their youngest daughter-in-law had gone to the south with their second daughter and son-in-law to participate in some training.

This news had accidentally reached his uncle's ears a few days ago, so he discussed it with his wife and little sister and came to a conclusion: Li Daoping had taken his wife and Li Mu's second aunt to the south to do multi-level marketing.

Unexpectedly, just a few days after they came to this conclusion, the three of them came back. As soon as they came back, Li Daoping invited them to Haizhou Restaurant again, and it was 888 yuan.

Before Li Mu's uncle, Xiao Yunjian, went out to the banquet, his face was full of doubts. When he bent down to put on his shoes, he only wore one leather shoe. He stood up, looked at his wife, Cai Yanhong, and said, "Hey, I'm really curious. What is Li Daoping doing?

Some time ago, I heard that the two of them applied for layoff, and then they went to the south with their second daughter-in-law. No one knew what they were doing there for half a month. Their second daughter-in-law quit her job and followed them. Do they really have a way to make money in the south? "

Cai Yanhong curled her lips and looked disdainful. "Haven't you heard?

The south is full of gold!

But that was said more than a decade ago when the reform and opening up was just started. How can money be so easy to make now? "

"But you can't stop him from spending!"

Xiao Yunjian frowned. "The last time we ate, I secretly asked the waiter. The meal, including cigarettes and alcohol, cost a total of more than 6,000 yuan. This time, it's three eights again. It's estimated that it will cost another few thousand yuan. If Li Daoping has no money, how can he be willing to spend it?"

Cai Yanhong was stunned for a moment, but she still nodded and said, "You're right."

After that, Cai Yanhong's expression was no longer disdainful. Instead, she said with some jealousy, "Say, that child Li Mu is also strange. I heard that he only scored about 500 points in the mock exam. How did he do so well in the college entrance examination and get into Renmin University …"

Xiao Yunjian laughed twice. "Maybe the Li family's ancestral grave is emitting smoke."

Cai Yanhong stretched out her hand and said, "Alright, don't be jealous. Hurry up and put on your shoes."

…

This time, the family members came on time. Because Papa Lee and Li Ma had informed in advance that it was to celebrate Li Mu's admission to Renmin University, the relatives came with red envelopes. As soon as they entered the door, they gave the red envelopes to Li Mu, congratulating him for getting into such a good university.

Li Mu did not reject the elders' kind intentions. This kind of money was the same as red envelopes for future marriages. They were all money between relatives, so naturally, they should be exchanged.

At 6: 30, everyone had arrived. Zhou Gang, who did not come last time, also came. Except for Li Mu's cousin, Xiao Yuanyuan, who was not there.

First Aunt said helplessly, "We can't control Yuanyuan. We just bought her a camera, and she went on a trip to Qingdao with her friends."

Youngest Aunt said with a smile, "Sister-in-law, you can't always spoil Yuanyuan. It's okay for a girl to spend money, but she can't always run away."

First Aunt spread out her hands. "Even your brother can't control her, let alone me."

Li Mu's grandmother said with a look of relief, "Our three children who took the college entrance exam all got into universities. Yuanyuan is a first-tier university, Xiao Wei is a second-tier university, and this time Mu got into a key university. The next one is up to Haohao!"

The Xiao Wei that grandma mentioned was Li Mu's aunt's cousin, Zhou Wei. He did not come today. It was said that a classmate found him a part-time job, and he went to Yang City at the end of July to experience life.

Li Mu's uncle teased his son. "Haohao, I don't expect too much from you. I'll be satisfied if you can get into a second-tier university in Yanjing."

"Isn't it easy to get into a second-tier university?" Xiao Hao curled his lips and asked, "But why do I have to go to Yanjing?"

"To go to your cousin, idiot!"

Youngest Uncle laughed and scolded, "Your cousin is so capable. If you follow him in the future, won't you have a good life?"

Li Mu's uncle knew Li Mu's ability to make money, and he also knew that his wife's store manager was run by Li Mu alone. He really saw this nephew as a genius.

Xiao Hao heard this and suddenly understood. He looked at Li Mu, who was not far from him, and thought about the 100 yuan that his cousin generously gave him last time. He used that 100 yuan to treat the class belle, Nana, to a meal at KFC, and then took her to the park to ride a boat. On the way home, he successfully held her little hand. That feeling was indescribable.

Papa Lee was obviously different from before. Not only did he look radiant, but even his speech was more imposing than before. He picked up a glass of Maotai, stood up, and said with a smile, "Mom, Dad, Brother, Sister, Little Brother, Little Sister, thank you for coming today. On behalf of our family, I would like to propose a toast to everyone."

Youngest Uncle hurriedly said, "Second Brother-in-law, you can't propose a toast. We have to drink together."

Then, Youngest Uncle stood up and said happily, "Xiaomu is the first person in our family to be admitted to a key university. Let's raise our glasses together and wish Xiaomu a bright future!"

Eldest Uncle nodded and echoed happily, "Yes, that's right. I wish Xiaomu a bright future!"

Eldest Uncle and Eldest Aunt were a little jealous. A few years ago, when their daughter left, they did not see them so excited. Anyway, their daughter also left a university, which was great!

It was true that Li Mu was admitted to a key university, but did it mean that he would have a bright future in the future?

Look at their flattering faces, it was as if he could become a mayor or a governor after graduating from a key university.
