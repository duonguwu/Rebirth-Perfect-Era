Chapter 1684

After flying for more than ten hours, Li Mu's private plane landed smoothly at Sacramento International Airport.

Although the media knew that Li Mu was coming to the United States, they did not know Li Mu's actual itinerary, and the media did not know whether Li Mu would arrive in Los Angeles or Sacramento first.

In the past, when Li Mu came to the United States, he had always chartered a plane from a business jet company under Air China. Each plane was basically a different model and a different serial number, so it was not convenient for the media to capture it. Not to mention that he had changed to his newly bought Gulfstream G5 this time.

After Li Mu's Gulfstream G5 landed in Sacramento, Schwarzenegger, who had received the news in advance, had already quietly waited in a hangar at the airport. Not only did his assistant and staff come with him, but Celine Dion, who had just rushed over from Canada, was also with him.

In order to run for governor, Schwarzenegger had already bought a villa in Sacramento in advance, so he invited Li Mu and his party to stay at his house. Even Celine Dion and her husband were arranged to stay at his house.

Schwarzenegger felt that if Celine Dion also stayed at his house, she would be able to have more contact with Li Mu's sister. This would not only show that he was very good at doing things, but it would also be a great help to Celine Dion. If Celine Dion could have a good relationship with Li Mu's sister, she would definitely be able to get a lot of resources in the future.

Li Mu did not know that Schwarzenegger had already brought Celine Dion to the airport to pick them up, and Du Wei, who was kept in the dark, was even more unaware. She was now looking forward to seeing Celine Dion in person at Schwarzenegger's inauguration and taking a photo with her at the celebration party.

After the plane landed, it slowly drove into a semi-enclosed hangar under the guidance of a guide car. At this time, the hangar had been completely cordoned off by dozens of security personnel.

Interestingly, Schwarzenegger had not yet been sworn in, so even if he won the election, he could not use any public resources in California for the time being. Therefore, these security personnel responsible for guarding the scene were all hired from professional security companies by Schwarzenegger out of his own pocket.

The plane taxied into the parking lot under the California evening sun and slowly came to a stop.

Li Mu stood up from the luxurious reclining seat and stretched. Although the interior decoration of this Gulfstream G5 was extremely luxurious, the interior space was not big enough, so there was no special bedroom. After lying down for more than ten hours, he was a little exhausted.

Unlike Li Mu, Du Wei seemed to be in high spirits. She had been listening to Celine Dion's songs and Michael Jackson's songs along the way. At the thought of having a chance to meet her idol, she was so excited that she had long forgotten her tiredness.

Li Mu understood Du Wei's behavior very well. Practitioners in a field would always be full of supreme worship for the supreme god of the field. In today's pop music, the number one was naturally Michael Jackson. Even if Celine Dion was not ranked second, she would still be in the top five.

When the plane had just stopped, Du Wei's side of the porthole happened to be on the other side of the cabin door, so she did not see Schwarzenegger, Celine Dion, and the others. However, Li Mu could see clearly from the window on his side. He saw Schwarzenegger and saw a blonde middle-aged woman standing next to him. She looked a little familiar, but he was not sure who she was.

Li Mu did not have much feelings for Celine Dion. When "My Heart Will Be Forever" was popular all over the world, when he heard this song, he naturally thought of Jack and Meatballs in the movie. As for what Celine Dion looked like, he really couldn't think of it at once.

However, Li Mu suddenly thought of a certain Spring Festival Gala in his previous life. Celine Dion seemed to have sung a song with a famous singer, Hua Xia, on the same stage. Although Celine Dion had aged a lot at that time, her facial features and spirit matched.

Therefore, Li Mu concluded that the woman standing next to Schwarzenegger under the plane was Celine Dion.

He couldn't help but look at Du Wei. She was packing up her Mango player and headphones. At the same time, she gently shook her head and hummed Celine Dion's classic song, "I Love You".

"Surrender".

Speaking of this song, Li Mu had a deep impression of it, mainly because this song had been covered in Chinese by the Taiwanese singer, Lin Zhixuan, by Hua Xia. It was called "Never Left". When she participated in a singing show, this song was popular for a period of time.

Looking at Du Wei's focused look, Li Mu secretly wondered if Du Wei would be surprised to see Celine Dion when the cabin door opened later.

But then again, look at Schwarzenegger's burly and muscular appearance. Who would have thought that he would actually invite Celine Dion to pick him up at the airport with him?

At this time, the plane stopped and the engine gradually stopped. The Air China flight attendant opened the cabin door and said to Li Mu and the others, "Li Zong, distinguished passengers, you can get off the plane now."

Li Mu nodded, stood up, and said to Du Wei, "Let's go. They're already waiting to pick us up."

Du Wei asked curiously, "Who?"

Li Mu said with a smile, "You'll know when you get off the plane. Let's go."

Du Wei followed Li Mu to the cabin door. Li Mu took the lead to go down the spiral staircase. As soon as he walked down a few steps, Du Wei followed him down the stairs.

At this time, she unconsciously looked down and was suddenly stunned. She even forgot to go down the stairs.

At this moment, she happened to meet Celine Dion's eyes.

When Li Mu saw Celine Dion for the first time, he couldn't remember what she looked like, but Du Wei had always regarded her as the biggest idol in the singing field, so she recognized her at a glance.

She was so excited that she immediately covered her mouth and widened her eyes. She had never dreamed that she would see her idol in person as soon as she arrived in the United States and before she even got off the plane. Moreover, her idol would come to the airport to pick her up!

Li Mu looked at her and saw that she was in a daze, so he turned back to support her arm and teased, "Don't get excited. Look at the road. What if you fall?"

Only then did Du Wei come back to her senses. She was so excited that even her voice was trembling. "Brother Li Mu, I'm not dreaming, am I?

That … That's Celine Dion! "

Li Mu said with a smile, "I know. The one next to her is Schwarzenegger. Why are you so surprised?"

Du Wei said incoherently, "That's Celine Dion!

She's my biggest idol!

Oh, no, my biggest idol is you. She's my biggest idol in the music field!

She's my favorite female singer!

Oh my god, how … how could she come to pick us up? "

Speaking of this, Du Wei looked at Li Mu and asked, "Brother Li Mu, did you inform her?

There seems to be no other possibility except for you … "

Li Mu nodded with a smile and said, "Before we got on the plane, I asked your sister Ziwei to send an email to Schwarzenegger, telling him that you like Celine Dion and asking him to help arrange it."

As he said this, Li Mu looked at Schwarzenegger and Celine Dion, who were smiling down below, and said to Du Wei, "You see, comrade Schwarzenegger is quite good at doing things."

Du Wei stammered, "Brother Li Mu, I'm so nervous …"

Li Mu said with a smile, "What are you nervous about?

Because you're nervous about meeting your idol? "

"Well …" Du Wei nodded seriously.

Li Mu asked again, "Then you said that I'm your biggest idol. Why aren't you nervous when you see me?"

Du Wei blurted out, "I'm nervous every time, okay …"

"Really?" Li Mu frowned. "Why can't I see that?"

Du Wei blushed and hurriedly said, "Oh, I'm just talking nonsense …"

Li Mu didn't ask much. He said with a smile, "Let's go. Don't make a fool of yourself. They're still waiting to shake hands with you."

Du Wei nodded, calmed down a little, and followed Li Mu down the gangway.

At this time, Schwarzenegger and Celine Dion came up side by side. Schwarzenegger first shook hands with Li Mu and said seriously, "Welcome to California, Mr. Lee. It's been a long journey. Thank you for coming all the way."

Li Mu smiled slightly. "It's not hard at all. It's such a happy event that you've been elected governor. Of course, I should come to celebrate."

"Thank you very much!"

Schwarzenegger said with a sincere face. Then he turned around and introduced Celine Dion next to him.

"Mr. Lee, this is Celine Dion, a very famous Canadian female singer. Ms. Dion has admired you for a long time."

Li Mu reached out to Celine Dion and said with a smile, "Hello, Ms. Dion. I've heard a lot about you. I've always liked your songs."

Celine Dion said humbly, "Thank you for your compliment, Mr. Lee. You can call me Celine."

"Okay."

Li Mu nodded and said, "Celine, let me introduce you to my sister, Du Wei. She likes you very much."

Celine Dion hurriedly took the initiative to reach out to Du Wei and said with a smile, "Hello, Du Wei. I heard that you're also a singer. If there's a chance in the future, we must cooperate!"

Du Wei said nervously, "I wouldn't dare. I'm just a student at the Conservatory of Music. I'm not a real singer yet."

Li Mu smiled and said, "Xiaowei has released a few singles, but she hasn't officially released them yet."

Celine Dion nodded and said with a smile, "There's no hurry to release them. If you're still short of songs, I can help you to ask famous musicians in Canada and the United States for songs. At that time, I can also introduce you to experienced top producers in North America to help you make records."

Du Wei hurriedly thanked her and said, "Thank you, Ms. Dion …"

Celine Dion hurriedly said, "You don't have to be so polite. We're all friends."

With that, she took the initiative to take Du Wei's arm and whispered in her ear, "I've prepared a small gift for you. It's all my albums since my debut. I've already signed for you. I hope you like them."

"Really?!" Du Wei said happily, "Thank you so much …"

Celine Dion said with a smile, "I've said you don't have to be so polite. Let's leave each other's contact information and keep in touch in the future. Besides, we all like singing, so we can discuss and exchange information."

Du Wei hurriedly nodded happily.

At this time, Schwarzenegger said, "Mr. Lee, let's hurry up and go through the customs formalities. I've already prepared a dinner at home to welcome you and Miss Du in the evening!"
