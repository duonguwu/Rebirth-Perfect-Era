Chapter 693

After lunch, Li Mu drove to the airport. After parking the car at the airport, he took Su Yingxue through the security check and met Song Liang, who had arrived earlier, in the VIP waiting room.

Song Liang brought a young male assistant with him. Li Mu was very familiar with the young man. His name was Chen Fang, and he had always been a trusted subordinate of Song Liang. He had been following Song Liang since he entered the industry.

This time, Song Liang booked a first-class cabin, and the flight was quite comfortable. When the plane landed at Jinling Airport, someone arranged by Song Liang drove to Jinling Airport to pick them up. This time, Song Liang arranged two Benz cars. He took one with his assistant, and Li Mu and Su Yingxue took the other one.

On the way to Haizhou, Li Mu asked Su Yingxue, "When we arrive at Haizhou, will your parents come out to pick you up?"

Su Yingxue said, "I didn't tell my parents that I would be back today."

Li Mu asked her curiously, "Why didn't you tell them in advance?"

Su Yingxue said, "Yesterday, my mother called me to ask when I could go back. I said that I would wait for a few days because I had something to do at school. In the evening, you said that you would be back today. I thought that I might as well not tell my mother. If they asked me when I got home, I would say that I wanted to give them a surprise."

Li Mu said, "Your aunt and uncle should have also told them, right?"

Su Yingxue shook her head and said, "I told them not to say anything. They just wanted to cooperate with me to give them a surprise."

Li Mu nodded slightly and said, "Then let the driver take you directly to the gate of the community?"

"Okay."

On the way back, Li Ma called Li Mu to ask about his schedule. She did not forget to tell him that his uncle had booked a hotel in Haizhou Hotel in advance, and the family was ready to go to the restaurant. She asked him to go to the restaurant as soon as he came back.

Although Li Mu had some prejudice against his uncle, after understanding the truth of family harmony, he no longer argued in his heart. After returning to Haizhou, he first asked him to send Su Yingxue home, then watched Su Yingxue enter the community, and then took a car to Haizhou Hotel.

When the Benz car drove to the gate of Haizhou Hotel, Li Mu saw his uncle and his cousin, Xiao Hao, standing at the gate. Xiao Hao was taller than the last time Li Mu saw him. He was looking around sneakily, as if he was waiting for Li Mu.

Xiao Hao was 13 years old this year, and was in the awkward period when he had not fully grown. He was dressed like a normal person, but he was thin, and there was a small tuft of black hair on his mouth, which made him look quite wretched.

Li Mu knew that this kid would grow taller quickly in the next few years of junior high. After gradually getting rid of the awkward period, he looked more and more handsome. Otherwise, with his current poor appearance, even if his father supported him to find a girlfriend, he would not have a chance at all, let alone impregnate his puppy love.

Li Mu asked the driver to stop in front of the two. After thanking the driver, he pushed the door open and got out. Xiao Hao was muttering to his father, "Dad, when is my brother coming?"

His uncle said, "Soon."

Xiao Hao stamped his feet, "Oh, I am so anxious."

At this time, Li Mu pushed the door open and got out of the car. He looked up and asked Xiao Hao, "What are you so anxious about?"

Li Mu's sudden appearance startled the two of them. Xiao Hao was the first to react, and he threw himself into Li Mu's arms and shouted excitedly, "Brother, you are back, I missed you so much!"

Li Mu greeted his uncle first, and his uncle said with a smile, "Your family is waiting for you upstairs, let's go up too."

"Okay."

Li Mu nodded, patted Xiao Hao, who was hugging his waist, and said, "Let's go, let's go upstairs to eat."

Xiao Hao got up, looked up at Li Mu, tiptoed, and put his palm on Li Mu's shoulder, and said with a strong air of a gangster, "Brother, I found you to be more handsome."

"Don't flatter me." Li Mu knew this kid's character too well. He had a glib tongue since he was young.

The three of them entered the gate of Haizhou Hotel together. Xiao Hao pulled Li Mu and said to his father, who was walking in front, "Dad, you go up first, I have something to say to my brother."

His uncle urged, "What do you have to say, little brat? Hurry up and go!"

Xiao Hao stood there and acted like a spoiled child, "Aiya, Dad, I really have something to say to my brother!"

Seeing this, his uncle hesitated for a moment, and said to Li Mu, "Little Mu, if this kid wants money from you, don't give it to him. Even if he says he is poor, ignore him. Your aunt and I usually give him a lot of pocket money."

Li Mu smiled and nodded, "Okay, I understand."

Xiao Hao made a face at his father, and after his father left, he looked at Li Mu with a pleading face and said, "Brother, can you help me sign up for a Sanda training class?"

Li Mu asked in surprise, "Why do you want to sign up for that?"

Xiao Hao made a fist and said, "To practice Sanda!

The Children's Palace opened a Sanda training class, and the coach said that he won some Sanda championship in the province. I want to sign up, but the tuition fee is paid for half a year, and it's 800 yuan. It's too expensive. My mom only gives me 10 yuan a day during the summer vacation, and when I have enough money, school will start. "

Li Mu laughed, "Can't you learn something else?

What do you want to learn Sanda for? Do you want to learn Sanda to pick up girls? "

Xiao Hao's expression was a little gloomy, and he said, "If I practice Sanda hard for two months and become a master, no one in school will dare to bully me."

Li Mu saw that his expression was not right, and he couldn't help but ask, "What's wrong, were you bullied at school?"

"Yes."

Xiao Hao lowered his head and said, "There is a kid in our class who is always looking for trouble with me. I fought with him twice, but I couldn't beat him."

Li Mu hurriedly asked him, "Are you hurt?"

Xiao Hao said, "They are all minor injuries. I was originally not afraid of him in a one-on-one fight, but the key is that he has several friends to help him."

Li Mu asked, "He has friends, but you don't have friends?"

Xiao Hao kicked a small stone at his feet, and said with a distressed face, "I do, but they don't dare to help."

Li Mu shook his head helplessly, "You are careless in making friends. In the future, don't contact such friends."

Xiao Hao raised his head and said seriously, "I don't blame them. The key is that the guy's brother is a famous hoodlum in the high school section, and he often hangs out with the big brothers in the underworld. Previously, a guy in the third year of middle school looked for trouble with him, and his brother beat him up until he was hospitalized for a few days. Since then, no one dared to provoke him."

Li Mu hurriedly asked, "Did his brother hit you?"

Xiao Hao hesitated for a moment, then lowered his head and said, "Once during class, he came to the door of our class and slapped me twice."

At first, Li Mu thought that it was just a fight between children, a small matter that was not worth taking seriously. But when he heard this, Li Mu's heart tightened, and he felt that he must take this matter seriously.

He had also walked step by step from middle school to high school, and he had seen a lot of fights in school. Nowadays, the phenomenon of gangs on campus was very serious. It was common to become sworn brothers, form a small organization, and then gang up to bully other students. In his previous life, when Li Mu was in high school, he was also asked for protection fees. The so-called protection fees were actually a group of hoodlums who found honest students to "borrow money." Their words were very sophisticated. Borrowing money to save an emergency, they were friends, and if something happened in the future, they could mention their names, or directly ask for their help. But this money would definitely not be returned, and if something really happened, it would be impossible to ask for their help. But if they did not lend, then it was not giving face, and if it was light, they would be warned to be careful in the future, and if it was heavy, they might even be beaten on the spot.

In his previous life, Li Mu was quite a coward. Often when people "borrowed" three or five dollars, he did not dare to make a sound. Sometimes he was blocked by such people, and it was useless even if he did not want to borrow money. The other party would force him to search his body. Under normal circumstances, he did not dare to tell his teachers or parents, afraid that he would be retaliated. But in his heart, his teeth itched. Sometimes, his rarely seen manly spirit would suddenly emerge, and he would want to spend three dollars to buy a fruit knife to fight with the other party. But this kind of manly spirit generally did not last long, so the idea of fighting with the other party had always been there, but it had never been realized.

Because of this, Li Mu could understand Xiao Hao's feelings, and he also knew that Xiao Hao would definitely not tell his parents or teachers. If it was not for the fact that he felt that he could afford the tuition fees, he was afraid that he would not have told him either.

So, Li Mu asked him, "Is eight hundred dollars enough?"

"Yes." Xiao Hao nodded like he was pounding garlic.

Li Mu nodded. "I will accompany you to sign up tomorrow."

Xiao Hao immediately beamed with joy. "That's great, thank you, brother!"

Li Mu smiled slightly and asked him, "Do you know the name of that kid's brother?"

"I know, his brother is called Xu Hui, everyone calls him Huihui."

Li Mu asked again, "Who does he hang out with outside?"

Xiao Hao blurted out, "Brother Heizi."

"What does Brother Heizi do?"

"Fifth Middle School's Ximen Kaige, his boss is Brother Xiguan Erbin."

Li Mu slapped the back of Xiao Hao's head. "You brat, you don't learn well all day long. I don't see you doing well in your studies. You seem to understand the underworld quite well. Do you want to drop out of school and live in the underworld?"

This slap was not hard, and Xiao Hao still said with a wronged face, "I don't want to live in the underworld. What's so good about living in the underworld? That Huihui is doing well in school. He has to extort money from a junior high school student to buy soda on a hot day. It's so embarrassing."

Li Mu saw that he was speaking sincerely, and his heart was much more at ease. It didn't matter if he didn't study hard. With him around, he would have a way out in the future. If he wanted to be a gangster at such a young age, he really didn't know what was good for him.

Unexpectedly, Xiao Hao's next sentence was a whimper. "But there is one good thing about living in the underworld."

Li Mu asked him, "What good?"

Xiao Hao didn't even think about it and blurted out, "If you do well, you don't have to worry about finding a girlfriend."

"F * ck!" Li Mu slapped him again. "You don't learn well!"

Xiao Hao stuck out his tongue, rubbed the back of his head and said, "I'm telling the truth, all the little girls in school like that." After saying that, he said with a face full of sincerity, "Brother, don't worry, I definitely won't mingle around!"

Li Mu nodded. Although he promised to pay for him to learn Sanda, Li Mu didn't want him to really compete with others in school in the future. Thinking that Xiao Hao had just finished his first year of high school, and there were still at least five years of school to go in Haizhou. The campus in a small place was like a small world. He had to ensure that Xiao Hao would not be bullied in school for the next five years, so he said to Xiao Hao, "Tomorrow, I will help you solve the problem of Huihui and make him hide when he sees you in the future."

Xiao Hao blurted out, "Forget it, brother. I can solve it myself. You can just take me to the Sanda class to register tomorrow."
