Chapter 1374

When Li Mu stepped on the stage, the prelude of the music sounded.

It was the first time that he sang in front of the entire Chinese people, so Li Mu was a little nervous. However, he had been through a lot in the past two years. Although he was nervous, he adjusted himself in time, so his overall performance was not affected by his emotions at all.

After the prelude, Li Mu picked up the microphone and sang "A Loving Family" in his deep and magnetic voice, amidst the cheers and shouts of the audience.

"I like the warm lights waiting for me when I get home

I like to see everyone's smiling faces when I get up

I like to work hard for my family and my ideals when I go out

I like to share my happiness with you immediately

I like to think of your warm embrace when I get hurt

I like to think of your warm embrace when I get hurt

I like to think of how great it is for you to always accept me when I get angry

I like to bring back the good memories for you when I travel … "

Li Mu had a good sense of music, and his voice could be considered a karaoke master. Although his overall ability was not as good as those famous singers, he was much better than most idol singers. In addition, the audience did not have high expectations for Li Mu's voice, so when he started singing, the audience was stunned.

Li Mu sang two parts of the main song, and when he was about to enter the chorus, Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang suddenly went on stage from both sides of the stage. Just as the chorus began, the two walked towards Li Mu and sang with Li Mu at the same time:

"Because we are a family

A loving family

Only those who are fated to meet

Only those who are willing will cherish

Why let the dark clouds cover our eyes … "

Originally, Li Mu's voice had already surprised the audience, but they did not expect that Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang would also go on stage to sing with Li Mu. A king, a queen, and a genius. Such a lineup was quite strong.

The core idea of this song was in the chorus. It used the plainest language to express a simple attitude: whether it was the mainland, Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, the three places on both sides of the Straits were a loving family.

When the second part of the theme song began, the first line of the lyrics, "I like to forget all the messy feelings as soon as I get home," was sung. Along with the voice, Zhang Xueyou, one of the Four Heavenly Kings of Hong Kong, walked out from one side of the stage. The audience burst into applause and cheers again. At this time, the second line of the lyrics was sung, and from the other side of the stage came the mainland singer Wang Fei, who was known as the Queen of Music.

Wang Fei's second appearance caused a series of screams. At this time, the singer who sang the third line and went on stage was Zhou Jielun, a popular young singer from Taiwan.

The fourth line was sung by Liu Tianwang from Hong Kong.

The fifth line was sung by Tian Zhen, a powerful singer of the mainland.

The sixth line was sung by the veteran Taiwanese singer, Fei Yuqing.

The seventh line was sung by Hong Kong female singer Miss Rong.

The eighth line was sung by the mainland group Yu Quan.

The first line of the second part of the chorus was sung by four singers, Liu Xiaoying, Wang Lihong, Chen Xiaochun, and Li Jian.

For the audience, today was really an eye-opener. Familiar faces kept appearing on the stage. Every line of the lyrics would attract many singers, and they were all well-known singers from both sides of the Straits. In a short while, dozens of well-known singers stood on the stage.

As the accompaniment continued to advance, when the chorus ended, the entire accompaniment band raised the pitch by a key. Then, more than ten singers sang a line of lyrics, and more than ten well-known singers sang a line of lyrics. This was simply an extremely extravagant audio-visual feast. Even the Spring Festival Gala did not have such a grand display.

At this time, the audience sitting in front of the TV, especially those who were watching with their family and friends, were all exclaiming, "Aiya, Zheng Yijian!

Aiya, Chen Lin!

Aiya!

Zhang Baizhi!

Aiya … "

As the chorus continued to rise, more and more singers appeared on stage, and the atmosphere of the song became more and more heated. When all 178 singers appeared on stage, the stage was already extremely spectacular. The appearance fees of these celebrities added up to at least several tens of millions, if not 100 million. And now, for the sake of a song, they were standing on stage and doing their best to perform their best. This scene was truly unprecedented in the entire entertainment industry!

Interestingly, from the moment Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang appeared on stage, the two of them stood on either side of Li Mu. Therefore, the singers who appeared later also appeared on either side of the stage. So, until all 178 singers appeared on stage, Li Mu was still standing in the middle of the stage.

As more and more people appeared on stage, Li Mu wanted to quietly retreat to the back of the stage. But just as he was about to put this idea into action, Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang blocked his way on both sides. Mei Yanfang even reached out to hold Li Mu's arm, refusing to let him retreat no matter what.

For Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang, they knew very well that it was Li Mu who made it possible for 178 singers from both sides of the Taiwan Straits to sing the song "A Loving Family." Without him, today's performance would not have reached such a shocking scene. As the real promoter, Li Mu was the real core character on stage. Since he was the core character, he had to stand at the front and center of the stage. This was the honor that Li Mu deserved.

As the song "A Loving Family" came to an end, Li Mu's heart was filled with a sense of achievement. Originally, he wanted to help Mother Yang. Since Mother Yang had the idea of promoting the three regions of the Taiwan Straits to fight against SARS, he could not sit back and watch this idea go to waste. Therefore, he used his own favor to invite Zhang Guorong and Mei Yanfang back to China to save the situation. Then, he used all his resources to promote this performance. But at that time, he did not expect that the performance would eventually develop to such a scale.

The performance on the stage has entered the last chorus.

In the last time, the overall rhythm of the song has begun to slow down, and the accompaniment has gradually weakened. Li Mu and 178 actors sang the classic lyrics together:

Because we are a family

A loving family

Blessings should be enjoyed together, and difficulties must be endured together

Knowing and staying together, in exchange for … eternal … heaven …

The music gradually came to an end. As all the instruments ended at the same time at the last moment, all the lights on the stage lit up in an instant. 178 singers and Li Mu bowed to the audience below the stage and the audience in front of the TV.

The cheers of the audience almost overturned the entire recording studio, and the audience in front of the TV was also excited beyond measure.

On the stage, Li Mu and 178 singers bowed for a minute. The two hosts of CCTV, as well as Chen Wan and He Wan, who came from Xiangnan Satellite TV to support them, walked onto the stage at the same time. They could not hide their excitement and invited all the representatives of entrepreneurs, medical staff and family members to come on stage again.

The host first represented all the cast and crew, as well as all the audience and Hua Xia compatriots, and once again solemnly thanked all the medical workers and their families who were fighting on the frontline of the fight against SARS.

All the singers present, as well as the representatives of entrepreneurs, including Li Mu, paid their highest respects to them.

Then, the host thanked all the entrepreneurs who donated to the fight against SARS.

Then, the host thanked all the singers who performed on the stage today, especially those singers from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, who came to support the performance from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, despite knowing that there was a SARS epidemic in Beijing.

What was touching was that the four hosts took turns to thank all the singers and stars who participated in the performance. Every time a singer's name was mentioned, half of the more than ten cameras on the scene would give a close-up shot of the singer, and every singer who was named would step forward and bow to thank them.

Every one of the one hundred and seventy-eight singers received this courtesy.

As the host announced the successful conclusion of the charity gala against SARS, the atmosphere at the scene once again reached its peak.

Backstage, the lighting and sound engineers of Central TV high-fived each other to celebrate the successful conclusion of the performance. The lighting and sound system did not have any problems from beginning to end.

The live broadcast team was also high-fiving their colleagues, thanking everyone for their hard work. This live broadcast of the gala, which had only been prepared three days in advance, did not have any signal problems throughout the whole process, ensuring that the entire country's audience, as well as the global Chinese audience, could watch the entire performance without being affected.

The director and the staff of the broadcast team took off their headphones and walkie-talkies. Everyone hugged each other and cried. This was a performance of great significance. As the ones who controlled the overall situation, they felt a greater sense of accomplishment than the other staff members.

As the chief director of the entire performance, Director Xu was so excited that even his hands were trembling. His heart was hanging in the air the whole time, and now that he had finally calmed down, he felt that everything was so unreal. Everything had gone too perfectly, so perfectly that he could not believe it was real. He even thought that he was dreaming.

What Director Xu did not know was that today, all the senior executives of Yang Ma were in the station, watching the live broadcast of this performance together.

Originally, this performance was not that important to the station. Even if it was the Spring Festival Gala, it was impossible for all the senior executives to work overtime to watch it. However, because of Li Mu's help, this performance was given a much more important meaning. Step by step, this gala became more and more important. It was so important that in the eyes of the senior executives of the television station, this performance had surpassed all the other galas this year, surpassing the super highlight of the Spring Festival Gala, and becoming the pinnacle of Central TV's gala!

The scene of the performance was extremely grand, and the performance was extremely important to the global Chinese population. Not only did they pay special attention to such a performance, they were also extremely worried, afraid that there would be any abnormalities that would make the performance less than perfect.
