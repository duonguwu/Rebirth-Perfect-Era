The discount was borne by Le Tao Express itself, but it didn't really cost Boss Ma anything. It just tightened the profit of additional orders, so Boss Ma almost didn't feel any pressure when it came to implementing the discount.

Le Tao's coupon policy was also very sneaky. No matter how many stores the buyer bought from, as long as the coupon was used, the coupon would be split evenly according to the value of the goods in each store. For example, if a user bought a 50 RMB item in Store A and a 100 RMB item in Store B, they spent a total of 150 RMB. If they used the coupon to deduct 20 RMB, then Store A, which accounted for one-third of the total sales of the order, would bear one-third of the discount, which was 6.67 RMB. Store B, on the other hand, would bear two-thirds of the discount, which was 13.3 RMB. For the sellers, this part of the profit reduction was targeted at new customers, so it was beneficial for them.
