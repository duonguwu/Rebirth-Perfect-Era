Li Mu did not care about this kind of public opinion attack at all. He handed the problem over to the public relations department and asked them to write a light statement and make a push. The content of the push was also very concise and to the point. There were two main points in total: First, some websites vented their anger on Makino Technology because of YY123's position adjustment, and thus launched public opinion attacks and slander against Makino Technology. Their behavior was despicable and dirty, and they were really villains in the Internet field. Second, YY123 was a subsidiary website wholly owned by Makino Technology. Makino Technology had complete autonomy to make changes and adjustments to YY123 in any form. No company or website had the right to interfere. Websites that took advantage of this were purely improper and unreasonable.

After these two points of view were released, the huge water army on Zhao Kang's Douban also followed suit. Not only did they criticize the three major portals on YY and the third Fang Ping platform for having bad intentions, but they also spread this kind of opinion to the three major portals' own positions.

Throughout the whole incident, Makino Technology occupied the commanding heights of public opinion. Moreover, Makino Technology's ability to distribute information and mobilize the water army was much stronger than these portals. Therefore, these portals were quickly defeated. The most interesting thing was that even Li Mu did not expect that the other well-known websites would choose to support Makino Technology in this matter without exception. Each of them attacked the opinions and practices of these major portals, completely isolating them in the online public opinion.

Later, when Li Mu carefully thought about this problem, he quickly figured out the reasons for the other websites' actions. For them, such a big matter was necessary to speak up. The key was how they should speak up and which side they should stand on. If they stood on the side of the major portals, then they would undoubtedly be standing on the opposite side of Makino Technology. However, when he thought about Sina's experience, who else would be willing to offend an industry giant like Makino Technology?

They were all in the business of making websites, and the larger websites were all included in YY123's list. All of them received traffic from YY123, and they all relied on YY to spread their websites. Even if Muye Technology were to randomly block their websites, it would still bring them huge losses. Therefore, in this case, they had to stand on Muye Technology's side and speak up.

To objectively evaluate this incident, Makino Technology and the other portals should have their own rights and wrongs. Although some of Makino Technology's actions were legal, in the eyes of the audience, they might not necessarily think that it was very reasonable. On the other hand, although the other portals were facing a crisis, they criticized Mu Ye and Hai for being too extreme and obviously selfish and unfair. If there was no conflict of interest involved, the media would have their own opinions and would not be so unified. However, most of the online media did not pay attention to the objective view of the incident from the beginning. They only cared about interests. Just like some automotive vertical media, every seemingly fair and impartial car model comparison published by the official website almost always involved interests that the readers could not see. In the end, even if no one paid them to say good things about themselves, there would still be people paying them to smear their competitors' cars. In the end, it was not to let the readers clearly distinguish between the good and the bad, but to subtly influence the audience's buying tendency.

It was precisely because of this that Makino Technology received the support of the entire internet. Not only did the few portals that stirred up trouble not win the support of the public, but they were also isolated and collectively spurned by the netizens.

The fact that someone was secretly investigating him had always been a thorn in Li Mu's heart. Xu Jianjun was not willing to reveal too much, but Li Mu did not know any reliable private detectives, so he never had a chance to get to the bottom of this matter. Li Mu also looked for Xu Jianjun and vaguely told him that as long as he told him the details, he would not mistreat him, and the issue of money could be discussed. However, Xu Jianjun seemed to have a very professional bottom line. He was unwilling to tell Li Mu who was investigating him.

However, Xu Jianjun also revealed a very important point to Li Mu: the person who secretly investigated him only checked all his paper files and information. That was to say, there were only three channels for the other party to infiltrate at present: household registration information, school registration information, and business registration information. In addition, the other party did not investigate his whereabouts, followed him, investigated his address, and investigated the people he contacted in real life.

This made Li Mu slightly relieved, because at least it proved that the other party should only want to understand and grasp his situation, and not do anything harmful to him. Otherwise, the other party would definitely investigate his daily movements, where he went every day, how he went, who he usually contacted, what daily habits he had, and so on.

Xu Jianjun also felt that the person who secretly investigated Li Mu this time should not be too obvious to Li Mu, at least not for the time being. However, to be cautious, he still suggested that Li Mu should assign a few personal bodyguards as soon as possible to protect his personal safety.
